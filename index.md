# Family Chat privacy policy

**Effective:** November 4, 2026

Family Chat is a private app for families: a shared chat, calendar, lists, chores, meal planner, photo album, family board, check-ins and a family map. It is made by Christopher Fennell ("I", "me") for his own family and the families they invite. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to remove it.

**The short version:** what you put in the app is shared only with the members of your family (or of the chat room you post in). It is stored with Google Firebase so it can sync between phones. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and the app has no ads or analytics.

## What the app collects

**Your account**
- Your name and email address, from Google sign-in or the email and password you sign up with.
- Your profile photo and colour, if you choose them.

**What you share with your family**
- Chat messages, photos, videos, files, polls, reactions and pinned messages, in the family chat and in chat rooms.
- Calendar events, lists, chores, recipes and meal plans, family board notes and album photos.

**Location (only if you turn it on)**
- **Check-ins:** when you check in, or are asked to and agree, the app shares your location and the address near it. Check-ins are deleted automatically after 30 days.
- **Share my location:** if you turn this on for a family, the app shares your current location with that family about every 5 minutes, and sooner when you move. That includes while the app is closed (background location). The latest location is kept so the family map can show where you are. Your recent path is kept for 2 days, then deleted automatically.
- **Places and automatic check-ins:** if you save places (like "Home" or "School") and turn on automatic check-ins, the app notices when you arrive at or leave them and checks in for you.
- **Locate requests:** a family member can ask where you are. Your phone answers only if you have turned on sharing for that family.
- **Emergency SOS:** sends your location to your family straight away.
- You can turn location sharing off at any time, in the app or in your phone's settings.

**Battery level.** Check-ins include your phone's battery level and whether it is charging. The app also tells your family when your battery drops below 10%, with your location only if you share it with them.

**Device information.** A notification token for your phone, so it can receive notifications. The app does not collect your contacts, call history or advertising ID.

## Who can see it

- Your **family**: only the members of a family can see what is shared in it. In a chat room, only the people in that room can see it.
- **Me**, as the developer: only where needed to keep the app running or to help when asked, for example to delete an account.
- **Service providers** that run parts of the app:
  - **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions and Cloud Messaging) stores the data, signs you in and sends notifications. It runs on Google servers in the United States. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Firebase's privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
  - **Google Play services** works out your location on the phone, and Android's address lookup turns locations into addresses.
  - **OpenStreetMap** provides the map pictures. Your phone downloads them directly, so OpenStreetMap's servers see your IP address and which part of the map you're viewing, not who you are. See the [OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).
  - **Websites you import recipes from**: when you import a recipe from a link, your phone downloads that page from the website.

Your information is **never sold** and **never shared for advertising**.

## Security

Everything is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The data is stored encrypted by Google, and the app's server rules only let family members (and room members) read their family's information.

## How long it is kept, and deleting it

- Check-ins are deleted after 30 days, and location history after 2 days.
- Everything else is kept until it is deleted. You can delete your own messages, photos, events, notes and other items in the app at any time.
- **Leaving a family** removes you from it and its chat rooms. The things you shared there stay visible to the family unless you delete them first.
- **Deleting your account:** email the address on Family Chat's Google Play page and ask for your account to be deleted. Your account, your profile and the items you created will be deleted within 30 days.

## Children

Family Chat is meant to be used within a family. Children should use it only with a parent or guardian's permission, as part of a family the parent belongs to. It is not designed for or marketed to children on their own. If you think a child is using the app without a parent's permission, get in touch (see Contact) and their account will be deleted.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
