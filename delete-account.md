# Delete your Family Chat account

Family Chat is made by Christopher Fennell. You can delete your account and your data at any time.

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open Family Chat and tap your picture at the top right.
2. Scroll down to **Settings** and tap **Delete my account**.
3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

## Without the app

If you no longer have the app or can't sign in, email the developer at the address on Family Chat's Google Play page. Send it from the email address you use to sign in (or tell us that address) and ask for your account to be deleted. It will be deleted within 30 days, and you'll get a reply when it's done.

## What is deleted

- Your account and sign-in, your profile, name and photo
- Your chat messages and voice messages, and the photos, videos and files in them
- The photos you added to family albums
- Your check-ins, your location and location history, and your recorded drives
- Your settings, such as which alerts you get, and your phones' notification tokens

You are also removed from every family and chat room you're in. A family you were the only member of is deleted entirely.

## What is kept

Things you added to a family's **shared lists, calendar, chores, recipes, meal plan and family board** stay for the rest of the family, because they belong to everyone in it. You can delete any of them yourself before deleting your account.

Check-ins and drives are deleted after 30 days and location history after 2 days anyway, even if you keep your account.
